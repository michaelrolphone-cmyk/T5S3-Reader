empty-cage trial after the original balance puzzle has been solved and its
opening has finished. Admission checks the real conserved six loads, equal
torque and occupied brake; this scene never supplies weights or a solution.

Confirm approaches the cloth-covered middle of the existing handle. Hold
Right to lower the empty cage by up to 32 world pixels, release or press Confirm
to stop at any height, and hold Left to raise it. Every stroke is at most 96
fixed steps. The player can repeat the trial indefinitely; only the small
completed-cycle counter saturates at two. At the landing, Confirm looks along
the same drawn cable to its pulley, searching for loose wires. No new evidence,
reading credit, safety verdict or mandatory test gate is granted.

back within the same bounded stroke before returning control at the exact
entry position. The scene leaves all live player, puzzle, route, evidence,
read and ending fields unchanged. Pause, journal and controller faults stop
its clock, and neutral input is required after journal, return and recovery.
