# Chapter VI: cloth on the ledge; country behind

The first upper quarry bench now carries the novella's three small bootprints
in old lime, between larger adult impressions. The small row stays toward the
wall; a tied strip of cloth marks the exposed edge at a child's shoulder height.
The patch and its short ties sample the existing support-three contour. Neither
cloth nor tracks add a collision surface, rail, collectible or instruction.

While the player walks or stands beside the cloth, the ordinary articulated
silhouette places one reachable hand on the same world coordinate that draws
it. Turning back keeps the contact; jumping or returning to the ladder releases
it. Feet, body, momentum, ladder rungs and the original route remain physical.
The strip cannot carry the player's weight. There is no timed scene or input
handoff: the player can stop, turn and pass at their own pace.

Above the second ladder, the cuttings open onto the country already crossed.
The marsh is a pale, bending strip between darker banks; the oil tanks have
contracted to dull elliptical roofs; the railway is a single distant line.
The rock opening follows the near world while the landscape moves at quarter
speed. The real upper bench height occludes the view from below. The existing
camera, ledges, remaining ladder, balance controls and cage remain unchanged.
The vista does not show a house or claim that the route can now be retraced.
The narrator's separate notebook/failed house-search reflection is not enacted
by this scenery increment.

This is the cumulative unreleased Hollow Trail 1.1.49 → 1.1.50 change in PR436;
minimum firmware stays 1.3.37. No API, controller mapping, evidence, reading,
puzzle, ending or saved-state format changes are required. The stone-pouch,
scarf-grip, optional empty-cage trial and earned hoist/gallery scenes retain
