# U1 glanceable progress reporting

**Mandatory; September 18, 2026.** Read [Three-Step Implementation-First Workflow](IMPLEMENTATION_FIRST_QUALIFICATION_WORKFLOW.md), [U1 Continuation Protocol](U1_CONTINUATION_AND_COMPLETION_PROTOCOL.md) and [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md). The owner may glance briefly. Give a few factual sentences: substantive code changed (commit/PR when confirmed), useful actual check/failure, and next work item or genuine blocker. Keep detailed logs in the concise ledger. Do not idle on CI, repeatedly discuss future hardware tests, or claim unrun checks passed.

**Absolute FINAL LINE of every U1 response:** ONE standalone Markdown-bold 2–5-word current-state label with nothing following it. These are the EXACT THREE owner workflow steps:

1. **Work Complete** — the first truthful announcement that ALL requested IMPLEMENTATION code is finished, integrated and free of known blocking defects. Put PR/commit and real checks/limitations ABOVE this final line. It is not a statement about release qualification, hardware tests, green CI or publication.
2. **Improving Code** — the NEXT and EACH LATER response to `continue` after Work Complete until the owner explicitly says to advance to qualification. Perform substantial independent audit, improvement, reusable focused test or defect repair on the same PR; **actively work, do not merely await the owner or repeat the completion report**. If a new blocking defect invalidates completion, return temporarily to **Implementation In Progress** until fixed and re-announce **Work Complete**.
3. **Release Qualification** — ONLY after the owner explicitly initiates joint qualification; test, debug, patch and establish the final revision/assets together, then report qualified only on real evidence and owner acceptance. Release-qualified acceptance is an OUTCOME of step three, not another step or status. Merge/flash/tag/publish require explicit instruction.

Before the first Work Complete event, use **Implementation In Progress** (a progress label, not a fourth workflow step). Genuine temporary blockers may override these labels with **Build Failure Under Repair**, **GitHub Quota Blocking Work** only on confirmed GitHub rate limiting, or **GitHub Access Unavailable** for unconfirmed connectivity. Explain blockers just above final line; do not invent a reset date. Retire former `Software Ready For Testing`, `Implementation Complete Awaiting Qualification`, `Post Completion Audit Active` and `Release Qualified Awaiting Authorization` statuses, which fragmented the owner's three-step workflow. Do not fabricate commits, test results, acceptance or background execution.
